Kestrel Yard — a round-based tactical FPS in the style Counter-Strike made famous, built on three.js. Two teams of five fight over a decommissioned container terminal at dusk: Vanguard (teal) plants the charge at site CRANE or SILO, Jackals (rust red) hold the sites. First to 5 rounds wins the match. Single-player against 9 bots with routes, holds, reactions, bursts, hearing, planting and defusing; a money economy with a buy menu (4 weapons, grenades, kevlar); headshot damage, recoil and spread that punish firing on the move, crouching, jumping and collision; a HUD with health, armour, ammo, timer, score, kill feed, banners and a 2D minimap; synthesized WebAudio sound; and pooled particle/tracer effects. All art is painted on canvas at load and all geometry is original — no copied map, characters or names.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
